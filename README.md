## The line

Why it works: a deadpan machine voice, smug about its own sensors; the turn is the vacuum trusting its log over the visible floor; the punch lands on the last sentence. No stock dust joke, no tired-appliance cliché — just a machine that would rather be wrong with its data than right with its eyes.
